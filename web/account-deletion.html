Delete your PartMo account

PartMo / Precision Automotives · Last updated: 21 July 2026

This page lets PartMo users request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data without reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Select the Request account deletion button below.
  2. Send the email from the address registered to your PartMo account.
  3. Keep the subject line PartMo Account Deletion Request.
  4. Include your registered name and phone number only if needed to identify the correct account.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email application, email precisiong@gmail.com with the subject shown above.

Identity verification

To protect accounts from unauthorised deletion, we may ask you to verify ownership of the registered email address or provide limited information needed to identify the account. Do not send your password, payment credentials, OTP, or complete banking information.

Data that will be deleted

After verification and completion of the request, PartMo will delete or anonymise data associated with the account, including where applicable:

  • Account credentials and authentication identifiers.
  • Name, email address, phone number, profile details, and profile photo.
  • Saved vehicle or garage details and uploaded vehicle photos.
  • Saved addresses, wishlist information, and other account preferences.
  • Other personal data stored solely to provide the PartMo account.

Data that may be retained

Limited order, transaction, invoice, fraud-prevention, dispute, or support records may be retained only when reasonably necessary to complete an active transaction or comply with applicable tax, accounting, consumer-protection, security, or other legal obligations. Retained information is restricted to those purposes and deleted or anonymised after the applicable requirement ends.

What happens next

We will acknowledge the request, perform any necessary ownership verification, and process it within a reasonable period. We will notify you when deletion is complete or explain if limited information must be retained. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting the app is not enough

Uninstalling PartMo, signing out, or stopping use of the service does not automatically delete your account. Use the request process on this page to initiate account and associated-data deletion.
